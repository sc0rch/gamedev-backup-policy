Personal backup utility

Gamedev Backups

Backups of selected files, stored in the owner's private Google Drive.

What the app does

Gamedev Backups is a private, personal-use desktop utility. It runs locally, packages selected files into ordinary archives, and uses rclone to transfer them to Google Drive. It can also retrieve backups for local restoration.

Why it connects to Google Drive

Google authorization lets the app create and manage its backup files in the owner's Drive. The app requests the drive.file permission, which limits access to files created by or explicitly made available to the app.

Privacy and contact

The backup software and its credentials stay on the owner's device. The app has no advertising or analytics. Read the Privacy Policy for details about data use, storage, and access controls.

For questions about Gamedev Backups, use the support email shown on its Google authorization consent screen.
