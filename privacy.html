Privacy Policy

Gamedev Backups · Last updated October 11, 2026

Purpose

Gamedev Backups is a personal-use backup utility operated by its owner. It runs locally, packages selected files into ordinary backup archives, and uses rclone to store and retrieve them in the owner's Google Drive.

Data accessed and used

The app reads the local files selected for backup. Through Google OAuth, it requests the drive.file permission to create, read, update, and delete backup files and folders created by or explicitly made available to the app.

It uses the contents and metadata of those Drive files, such as file identifiers, names, and sizes, only to upload, verify, maintain, and restore backups. Google access and refresh tokens are used to authorize these operations.

Storage and protection

The owner has chosen ordinary backup archives without a separate archive password. Access is controlled by the owner's Google Drive account and sharing settings. The app does not create public sharing links. OAuth credentials and tokens used to access Drive are kept locally on the owner's device and are not included in these archives.

Backup archives and their Drive metadata remain in the owner's Google Drive until removed by the owner's backup retention settings or deleted by the owner. Local files and credentials remain until the owner removes them.

Sharing and other services

The app does not sell data, use data for advertising, or include analytics. It does not share Google user data with other people or unrelated services. Google receives the backup archives and the metadata necessary to provide Google Drive storage and authorization, under Google's Privacy Policy.

This informational website does not access backups or Google authorization tokens and does not set analytics cookies. Its hosting provider may process routine request metadata, such as an IP address, browser information, and access time, to deliver and secure the website.

Control, revocation, and deletion

The owner can stop backups locally and revoke the app's authorization through Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents future authorized Drive operations; it does not delete existing backups.

To remove stored backups, delete the app's backup files in Google Drive and empty the Drive trash as needed. To remove local credentials or backup data, delete them from the device. Stop any scheduled backups first to prevent new copies from being created.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the app owner using the support email shown on the Gamedev Backups Google authorization consent screen.

This page will be updated if the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
